The Importance of Professional Training

Professional training ensures that you learn the right habits from the start. Many self-taught drivers develop "bad habits" that can be dangerous in high-traffic areas.

Top 3 Tips for Beginners

  • Stay Calm: Anxiety is the biggest enemy of a new driver. Take deep breaths and focus on one control at a time.
  • Look Ahead: Don't just look at the car in front; look further down the road to anticipate traffic changes.
  • Practice Consistently: Driving is muscle memory. Short, frequent sessions are better than one long session per week.

Whether you are learning on a Maruti Swift or a Brezza, the principles of safety remain the same. Focus on your surroundings, respect road signs, and always stay alert.
